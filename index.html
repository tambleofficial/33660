BRAND STORY

A Haeundae original made with Busan local ingredients

336:60 is located on the 1st floor of the CGV building on Haeundae Gunam-ro. The brand is built around a simple contrast: “Slow” for the 336 hours of dry aging, and “Fast” for the satisfying speed of street-food service.

Its representative ingredient is same-day butchered Gimhae local pork, dry-aged in-store for 336 hours to intensify flavor. The pork is then butchered and smoked in-house to create a smoky K-BBQ expression with richer aroma and texture.

The sauces also reflect regional identity. Using anchovy fish sauce from Gijang Daebyeon Port, 336:60 crafts its own cheese sauce and spicy sauce. The brand also reinterprets a Gangwon-style meat jeonbyeong through a Busan lens and serves fresh house-made soy milk, called Ttoujang, every day.
