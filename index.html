OUR STORY

Local ingredients. A Haeundae attitude.

LOCAL PORK

Our main ingredient is Gimhae local pork, prepared with an in-house 336-hour dry-aging process to concentrate savory flavor before it ever reaches the grill.

BUSAN FLAVOR

We use anchovy fish sauce from Gijang Daebyeon Port in our signature sauces, bringing a distinctly local layer to cheese and spicy flavors.

STREET FOOD

Inspired by Korean meat jeonbyeong, we rework the format into something generous, smoky and easy to enjoy while exploring Haeundae.
